Emotional Wellness Pillar

Scale Breakdown

Flourishing

Emotions are information, not disruption

Emotions feel like information rather than disruption. You move through hard feelings with more ease and recover faster than you used to. You know yourself well enough that most things don't catch you completely off guard.

What this might feel like:

You move through hard feelings without losing yourself in them, and you recover faster than you used to. You know yourself well enough that not much catches you completely off guard.

Thriving

Reading patterns before they escalate

Your emotional awareness feels like a real tool. You understand your patterns well enough to anticipate some of them, and you have strategies that actually work when things get hard. You're not just surviving difficult emotions, you're learning from them.

What this might feel like:

You have a few things that reliably help, and you actually use them. Difficult feelings still show up, but they feel like something you're working with rather than something happening to you.

Growing

Connecting feelings to their causes

You're building a clearer picture of what affects your emotional state. You're starting to connect feelings to triggers, situations, and patterns. Hard moments still happen, but you're more often able to name what's going on.

What this might feel like:

You're seeing patterns: certain situations, people, or times of day that tend to pull you one direction. Hard moments still land hard, but you're less confused by them than you used to be.

Emerging

Noticing feelings after they happen

You're starting to notice your emotions rather than just be swept up in them. There are moments of clarity: you catch yourself mid-reaction, or recognize a feeling after the fact. It's inconsistent, but the awareness is beginning.

What this might feel like:

Sometimes you catch yourself mid-reaction. More often you piece it together hours later. The awareness comes and goes, but it's there in a way it wasn't before.

Needs Care

Emotions feel reactive and confusing

Emotions feel like they're happening to you rather than something you have any say in. You might feel reactive, easily overwhelmed, or just flat, and not fully understand why. Hard days leave a mark that takes a while to shake.

What this might feel like:

You react before you realize what you're feeling, or some days you don't feel much of anything. Hard days stay with you longer than you want them to, and you're not always sure what set them off.
