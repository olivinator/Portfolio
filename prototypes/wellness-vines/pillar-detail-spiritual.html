What is Spiritual Wellness

What it reflects

Whether how you're spending your time actually reflects what matters to you, and what shows up emotionally when it doesn't.

What you might notice

Purpose-driven moments, like volunteering, helping someone, or acting on a value, are often linked to a same-day mood lift, even when nothing else about the day changed.

How it connects to your mental health

A strong sense of purpose is one of the most reliable predictors of emotional resilience and life satisfaction. Spiritual wellness doesn't require any particular belief system. It's about alignment: whether the way you're spending your time and energy feels connected to what actually matters to you. When that alignment is off, it often shows up as a persistent sense of emptiness or feeling like you're going through the motions.

What shapes this pillar

This comes through most clearly when you mention what felt meaningful, or the sense that something's missing, even if you can't quite name what.
